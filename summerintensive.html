Acting & Drama Intensive

A two-week immersion in the craft of theatre. Students work with their bodies, voices, and imagination to build presence and confidence. Session Journey — 13 Days

Day 1–2: Foundation & Play

Drama games, vocal warm-ups, and building ensemble trust to create a safe creative space.

Day 3–4: Voice & Expression

Focusing on speech clarity, tone, rhythm, and the art of expressive reading.

Day 5–7: Character & Story

Deep dive into character building, improvisation, and working through scripted scenes.

Day 8–10: Trinity Skill Tasks

Intensive practice of exam-specific tasks, including mime, poetry, and prose performance.

Day 11–12: Rehearsal & Refinement

Mock exam sessions and peer feedback to polish the performance to a professional standard.

Day 13: Showcase for Parents

Final performance for families, certificate reflections, and a celebration of the 13-day journey.

Why Trinity?

Trinity College London drama exams are recognised worldwide. A certificate is something a student carries in their portfolio for years — and working towards a real exam gives the whole experience a shape. Students prepare, take it seriously, and finish with something real to show for it.

Decision Making & Goal Setting Intensive

How to think clearly under uncertainty, and creating your own path in life, using mental models and techniques from world's best founders, entreprenuers and thinkers.

A 7-day intensive for students aged 10+, that teaches two skills that are extremely important: how to make decisions and how to set goals. Using mental models and evidence-based systems, students leave with a one-year plan and a personal system of goal setting and review they can actually use, year after year.

The Problem We're Solving

Every day, students face too many choices with no framework to handle them. They either freeze, follow someone else's opinion, or depend on parents for clarity. Drifing through without any clear direction of path of their own. Decision-making is a muscle, and right now, we are not really actively helping them build it.

But decision making is a skill. Goal setting is a skill. And skills can be learnt. This workshop will help the students to think clearly and decide with conviction, set a strong direction for themselves and set up real goals, and thereby take responsibility of their choices.

The Journey — 7 Days, 7 Tools, 1 Complete System

Day 1: Decision Audit

Look back at 3 decisions you've made. Find patterns in how you think and when you're at your best.

Day 2: Thought Experiments

Imagine two different futures before choosing one. Ask: "How could this go wrong?" before it does. Learn Inversion Thinking.

Day 3: Strategy of Approximation

Start with what you want. Work backwards through real constraints. Pick your 3 biggest goals for the year.

Day 4: Bottleneck Analysis

Find what's slowing you down. Build your personal system: what you stand for, how you work, what you won't give up.

Day 5: Leverage Decision

Find the one habit that makes everything else easier. Design it so it's impossible to skip.

Day 6: One-Way vs. Two-Way Doors

Learn which decisions need full commitment and which ones you can test first. Build a review habit.

Day 7: Atomic Decisions

Present your annual plan. Make a commitment. Leave as someone who owns their future.

What You Leave With — A Complete System

Four tangible tools students use for the entire year:

Bullet Journal — With their Past audits, 3 Rocks (top most goals), Decision Making Frameworks, Self Ritual design, and Robust Review System.

Annual Calendar — Full-year chart with movable sticky notes for goal dates. A living document, that can be modified in the future as needed.

One High-Leverage Ritual — So small it's impossible to fail. Small actions bringing Big Impact.

Review System — Questions for daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Acting & Drama Intensive
What ages is this for?

Ages 6–14, split into Junior (6–8) and Senior (8–14) cohorts. Both age groups run during both sessions (May 18–31 and June 1–14), so you can choose the dates that work for your family.

Is it compulsory for my child to appear for the Trinity Acting exam after attending the intensive?

No, it is not compulsory. This intensive is designed in alignment with the Trinity College London Acting curriculum, so your child will build the right skills and foundation. However, appearing for the exam and getting certified is completely optional. The decision is entirely up to you and your child.

Will this course give my child a participating certificate from Trinity College London?

No, this course does not provide participating certificate from Trinity College London. This is a preparatory intensive, which means it helps your child get ready for the Trinity exams. If your child would like to receive a Trinity certificate, they can register for an official Trinity exam (online or at a centre) and appear for the assessment separately. Our theatre educator can guide and support you through this process. After completing the intensive, your child will receive a certificate from the school.

Decision Making & Goal Setting Intensive
What ages is this for?

Ages 10+, with mixed cohorts. Younger students (10–13) get more guidance and visual tools. Older students (14–18) work independently and get leadership opportunities within the cohort.

My child is already smart. Is this still valuable?

Absolutely. This isn't academic tutoring. It's decision-making and goal-setting. Many high-achievers excel at following directions but struggle with independent direction. This teaches them to be the author of their own life, not just a high-performing follower.

What if my child doesn't know what they want?

Perfect. Day 2 is specifically designed for this — we use "Thought Experiments" to explore possibilities before committing. Many students come in uncertain and leave with clarity. The process itself reveals what matters to them.

Will my child get individual attention?

The Cohorts are group session, but we will be doing One-on-One sessions with the students either during the session itself or post the session, incase they need more help. These can be scheduled online as well.

General Questions
What if my child misses a day?

Each day builds on the previous. Missing one day is manageable (we provide catch-up materials). Missing two or more significantly impacts the experience. We recommend committing to all days.

Can my child do both programs?

Yes! Students 10+ can enroll in both. They get the full experience: decision-making in the mornings, theatre and drama in the afternoons.
