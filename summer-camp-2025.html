7 DAYS · PHYSICS-BASED MENTAL MODELS

Decision Making & Goal Setting Intensive

How to think clearly under uncertainty, using physics-based mental models

An intensive for 10+ year olds who want to learn how founders think. We use physics concepts as thinking tools — not because students need to become physicists, but because these models work. First Principles to strip away assumptions. Inversion to avoid pitfalls. Flywheels to build momentum. By the end, each student has a one-year plan for something that matters to them.

The Problem We're Solving

The world is getting more complex. Information overload. Option overload. Opinion overload. Schools teach compliance, not independence. Parents want to help, but they can't see inside their child's mind.

Students face decision paralysis (freeze or outsource to parents), goal confusion (passion without a path), and follow-through failure (systems beat willpower). This intensive teaches two timeless skills that cut through the noise.

The Journey — 7 Days, 7 Tools, 1 Complete System

Day 1: Decision Audit

Students map past decisions and learn: Good decisions ≠ good outcomes. They audit 3 decisions, identify patterns, and map their energy.

Day 2: Thought Experiments

"Travel" to two possible futures. Practice the Einstein method: simulate before deciding. Plus inversion: "How could this year fail?"

Day 3: Strategy of Approximation

Start with ideal vision, add constraints. Learn SMART vs. BHAG goals. Choose 3 Rocks through elimination rounds.

Day 4: Bottleneck Analysis

Map the week as a system. Find the constraint. Build a Personal Operating System: Vision, Mission, Values, Code of Conduct.

Day 5: Leverage Decision

Using the Four Levers (Labor, Capital, Code, Media), find where effort multiplies. Design ONE leverage-based ritual.

Day 6: One-Way vs. Two-Way Doors

Red Team exercise. Learn reversibility: commit to One-Way Doors, stay flexible on Two-Way Doors. Design a Review System.

Day 7: Atomic Decisions

Public pitch of Annual Plan. Formal commitment ceremony. "Do you witness my commitment?" Leave as architects of their lives.

What You Leave With — A Complete System

Not theory. Four tangible tools students use for the entire year:

Bound Annual Plan Journal — Past audit, 3 Rocks, Operating System, Ritual design, Review questions.

Living Calendar — Full-year chart with movable sticky notes for goal dates. A living document, not a prison.

One Leverage Ritual — So small it's impossible to fail. Trigger → Action → Reward.

Review System — Questions for daily (2 min), weekly (15 min), monthly (30 min), quarterly (1 hour), and yearly reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

[Acting + Theater] What ages is this for?

Ages 6–14, split into Junior (6–8) and Senior (8–14) cohorts. Both age groups run during both sessions (May 18–31 and June 1–14), so you can choose the dates that work for your family.

[Decision Making] What ages is this for?

Ages 10+, with mixed cohorts. Same exercises, different complexity. Younger students (10–13) get more guidance and visual tools. Older students (14–18) work independently and naturally mentor younger ones. The physics concepts work across all ages.

[Decision Making] My child is already smart. Is this still valuable?

Absolutely. This isn't academic tutoring. It's decision-making and goal-setting. Many high-achievers excel at following directions but struggle with independent direction. This teaches them to be the author of their own life, not just a high-performing follower.

[Decision Making] What if my child doesn't know what they want?

Perfect. Day 2 is specifically designed for this — we use "thought experiments" to explore possibilities before committing. Many students come in uncertain and leave with clarity. The process itself reveals what matters to them.

[Both Programs] What if my child misses a day?

Each day builds on the previous. Missing one day is manageable (we provide catch-up materials). Missing two or more significantly impacts the experience. We recommend committing to all days.

[Both Programs] Can my child do both programs?

Yes! Students 10+ can enroll in both. They'd attend the Decision Making & Goal Setting Cohort 2 (May 25–31) which overlaps with the second week of Acting + Theater (May 18–31). Or Cohort 4 (June 8–14) which overlaps with the second week of Acting + Theater (June 1–14). They get the full experience: theater in the mornings, decision-making in the afternoons.
